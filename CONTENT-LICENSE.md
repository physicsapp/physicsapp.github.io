## Changes made

- Learning objectives, key concepts, key equations, worked examples, and Check Your
  Understanding items were extracted from the OpenStax source files and reformatted
  for a phone app. End of chapter problems are not included.
- References to numbered figures and equations were reworded because numbers are not shown.
- Photos credited to third parties were removed. Diagrams and graphs made by OpenStax were
  kept and resized.
- New material was added: section summaries and quick review items (in `notes/`), using g = 10 m/s² for quick arithmetic.
- The full explanatory text of each section is not included; the app links to it on openstax.org.
